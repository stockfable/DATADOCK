Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. Everything you need to know about our enhancer.

How do I improve the quality of a photo?

You can crop, unblur, adjust colors, fine-tune lighting, boost saturation, or apply overlays. You can also clean up unwanted objects directly from your shot using our toolkit.

How does this tool enhance your photos?

Our engine sharpens the resolution by analyzing pixel data and rebuilding fine details. It focuses on clarity rather than changing the colors or lighting of your original image.

Why do people use an image quality enhancer?

Old or low-quality family photos often blur over time. Some user-generated content is too pixelated for professional use. Enhancing them brings back life and makes them ready for printing, social posts, or design projects.

What kind of photos do not work that well?

If the image is already high-resolution or heavily filtered, results may be subtle. Very blurry photos sometimes can't be fully recovered, and portraits generally respond better than scenery or text.

Can I use it on mobile phones?

Yes! Our tool works smoothly on both iOS and Android browsers. Just upload your photo and let the AI do its magic on the go.

What makes SP INHANCE the best enhancing tool?

SP INHANCE combines advanced AI algorithms with a simple, intuitive interface. Free to use, no sign-up required, and results that rival professional editing software.

Is SP INHANCE free?

Completely free for all users. No hidden fees, no watermarks. Download as many enhanced images as you want.

Will the file size change after enhancement?

Yes, since the AI adds extra pixel data during enhancement, the output file is usually larger than the original one. This is a sign that more detail has been added.
